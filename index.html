About

I’m Jaakko Väkevä, a researcher and photographer. Here you can find a selection collection of my academic and photography work.

Feel free to reach out regarding research discussions, potential collaborations, or photography enquiries!

Research

My research focuses on Games & Human-Computer Interaction. Particularly, I focus on how digital games can evoke various emotionally impactful, personally meaningful, resonating, and even transformative experiences.

I’ll share selected projects, publications, and other academic work here.

Photography

I am particularly into analog film photography, and experimenting with techniques such as multiple exposures and vintage filters, to capture dreamy projections of a world I see, or perhaps imagine to see. In my digital photography I enjoy playing around with different lighting setups and flash techniques, and often lean towards intense color palettes, drawing inspiration from popular culture and particularly early 2000's aesthetics.
